## Limitations and release rules

Visible query rows account for only a small fraction of the page/site clicks returned in this baseline. Google can withhold anonymised queries, and the connector does not expose an explicit completeness/pagination statement. Do not treat visible query rows as the full demand set or assume absence means zero demand. A small difference between summed page clicks and the site aggregate also means the site aggregate should be used for site totals, not sums of page rows.

Position means impression-weighted average position, not a fixed live rank. CTR must be computed from total clicks divided by total impressions when combining periods; do not average row CTRs. The URL-prefix property does not by itself establish coverage of other host/protocol variants. Search Analytics does not prove current index status or supply backlink evidence.

No new articles, rewrites, merges, redirects or culls are authorised by the numbers alone. The existing authorisation still governs implementation: research each topic before writing, retain truthful publication/update dates, use the queue and PR checks, and verify releases. Final merge/redirect destinations and permanent removals remain held until adequate site-specific search and backlink evidence supports them. Obtain longer history before treating an absent 90-day page row as a cull signal.
