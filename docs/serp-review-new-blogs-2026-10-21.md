- Intent/formats: learn a conversion method, recall useful equivalents, compare mixed forms and immediately check practice. Entities: numerator/denominator, place value, hundredths, equivalent fractions, simplifying, per hundred, fraction-as-division, exact/rounded values, ascending order.
- Brief: distinguish changing representation from finding a fraction/percentage of an amount; teach meaning and methods with an equivalence table, worked comparisons, decimal-place traps and original explained questions.
