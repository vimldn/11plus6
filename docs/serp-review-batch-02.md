- Retrieval: 10 am demonstration / electrical exclusion / waiting near windows / lack of storage / red box on desk / thread and sandpaper.
- Inference: conclusions are qualified and anchored in the rehearsal story; no claim that Nia is objectively the best musician or that future attendance is certain.
- Evidence: exact quoted phrases match the passage; lorry test success deliberately remains unknown. Own-words responses are not presented as direct quotations.

## Length decision

No claim of measured competitor word counts: retrieved page text included navigation, and PiAcademy’s article body was unavailable. Visible formats ranged from a short guide with two questions to longer multi-example resources; advertised reading times are not treated as word counts.

Chosen scope per page: a concise skill explanation, a complete original four-paragraph passage, three worked examples, five common mistakes and six questions with explanations. Length follows that teaching task, rather than adding filler to match a ranking page. Exact content string word counts can be recomputed with the project’s TypeScript runtime; browser labels, navigation and schema keys should be excluded.
