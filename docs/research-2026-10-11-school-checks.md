## 1. when-are-grammar-school-open-days-and-visits — rewrite

Queries: “grammar school open days 2027 visits Year 5”; “grammar school open days booking visit before 11 plus 2028 entry”.

Intent/format: find dates, book places and understand which school year can attend. Results include event directories and short school admissions/event pages. Read https://www.whichgrammar.co.uk/open-events : broad filterable listing, but event type/year needs checking and Sixth Form events can appear beside general admissions events. Useful breadth does not establish dates for the intended Year 7 cohort.

Official pages read: https://patesgs.org/Admissions/Year-7-11-Entry/ (10 February and 4 March 2027 mornings; booking details January 2027); https://challoners.org/Open-Events/ (28 January, 18 March and 1 July 2027; booking midday 14 January, 4 March, 17 June respectively); https://www.alcestergs.co.uk/page/?pid=110 (Year 5 evening June 2027, day not published); https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/schooladmissions/grammar-schools (2028-entry test timetable moves into Year 5).

Entities/relationships: school → Year 7 event → attendance/booking requirement; test year versus entry year; Year 5/Year 6; open evening versus ordinary-day tour; test registration versus school application versus offer-holder induction. Subtopics: booking, sold-out visits, accessibility, missed events and saving the source date. School pages are concise operational notices; directory is broader but less suitable as final evidence. Original value: a small explicitly checked 2027 table plus a reusable visit record, rather than an invented national calendar.

Remove fabricated national dates, county counts, school classifications, parking claims and universal private-visit promises. Keep URL and publication date. Distinct from the questions page: this page owns timing/booking. No merge or cull.

## 4. do-grammar-schools-provide-bus-or-transport-options — rewrite

Queries: “grammar school transport buses nearest suitable school free”; “grammar school bus transport paid coach late clubs eligibility”.

Read https://www.swindongrammarschooltransport.co.uk/faqs/ : detailed commercial booking FAQ covering payment, routes, service arrangements and cancellations; distinguishes booking intermediary/operator. This is evidence of questions families need to resolve, not proof of nationwide prices, reliability or future routes. Official read https://www.buckinghamshire.gov.uk/schools-libraries-and-parks/school-transport/free-school-transport/ : selective school choice does not itself make it the nearest suitable school; nearer upper/all-ability school can defeat ordinary entitlement.

Fresh official trust verification: https://www.gov.uk/free-school-transport ; https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-to-school-travel and its current PDF (cover dated May 2026) https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a10649929bb11891b1cf096/Travel_to_school_for_children_of_compulsory_school_age.pdf . Read paragraphs 1–16, 29–38 and relevant nearest-school/measurement/discretionary passages. Search1 included “site.gov.uk Home-to-school travel grammar”. Age 8+ normal distance condition is more than THREE MILES, not 3km. Nearest suitable is not “most academically suitable”; available all-ability provision matters. SEND eligibility assessed individually; an EHCP is neither always required nor automatic entitlement. Unsafe-route assessment considers accompaniment. For the 2026/27 academic year, extended rights use Targeted FSM (UC, household annual earnings no more than £7,400), not all Expanded FSM. For age 11–16, relevant three-nearest rule is more than 2 and not more than 6 miles. Parent copy keeps this compact and refers case assessment to the council, not a homemade distance calculation.

Intent/format: establish whether a workable route, paid seat and/or entitlement exist. Entities: home council → nearest suitable school → eligibility decision; school admission separate from transport; commercial operator/booking service; stops, timetables, annual contracts, clubs. Subtopics: normal-day routes, backup journey, SEND access, seat availability, paid cancellation terms. Official guidance is lengthy and technical; operator FAQ detailed and transactional. Original value: a route/entitlement/seat distinction and a door-to-door planning checklist.

Remove all invented operator/school routes, prices, capacities, county totals, reliability rates and grants; false 3km rule, universal straight-line measurement, automatic SEND/FSM entitlement and old deadlines. Remove international filler. Rewrite, preserve URL/date; no merge/cull. Future 2028 routes/prices explicitly unconfirmed.
