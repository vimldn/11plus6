# Batch 01: live search and editorial review

Research date: 7 October 2026. Status: **three drafts; no release approval implied**.

## Method and limits

Live web searches used: `11 plus fractions of amounts questions worked examples`, `11 plus ratio sharing questions`, `11 plus percentages of amounts questions`, followed by a second search provider for `11 plus fractions of amounts ratio percentages PiAcademy worksheets` and a government curriculum query. Opened topic competitors and primary curriculum pages to inspect their content. This is a dated search-result sample, not a Google UK rank-tracking export: no exact positions, search volumes or traffic estimates are asserted. Results vary by engine, location and time. Search snippets alone were not treated as verification of education or exam-format claims.

Content scope intentionally targets three precise learning intents rather than trying to reproduce broad fractions/ratio/percentages category pages. No competitor questions, illustrations or prose were copied. The resources contain original arithmetic problems. Basic methods were cross-checked against curriculum context and independently recalculated; no qualified educator approval is claimed.
