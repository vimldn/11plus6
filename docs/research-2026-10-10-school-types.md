## 2. are-grammar-schools-free-or-private

- Queries: `are grammar schools free or private fees England`; `grammar schools fees private tuition boarding voluntary contributions England 2026`; `grammar schools free fees private grammar parents`.
- Intent/format: direct cost answer, confusion over independent names, realistic budget. [Save My Exams comparison](https://www.savemyexams.com/learning-hub/useful-resources/grammar-school-vs-private-school/) opened: extensive comparison, fee section, name examples, FAQs and author/reviewer metadata. It makes some broad examples/claims that require independent verification. Opportunity: distinguish compulsory entrance test from paid optional familiarisation, tuition from boarding and voluntary contribution from an optional-extra charge.
- Entities: tuition, state/independent, academy, entrance-test fee, optional mock, uniform, transport, meals, charging/remissions, state boarding, VAT, bursary versus scholarship.
- Trust: [DfE answer 4Nov2025](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2025-10-14/82070/) explicitly prohibits entrance-test charges but permits optional familiarisation charges; [DfE charging guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charging-for-school-activities) and linked PDF read; [state boarding](https://www.gov.uk/types-of-school/state-boarding-schools); [VAT measure](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-on-private-school-fees/applying-vat-to-private-school-fees); [North London Grammar2026/27 fee schedule](https://northlondongrammar.com/school-fees/) proves independent grammar-name ambiguity, quoted only as current schedule example, not 2028 prices. Academy page confirms no fees. Charging guidance specifically addresses maintained schools; parent should inspect individual academy charging/remissions policy too.
- Remove false £20–80 entrance fees, national extras/fee averages, sibling tuition discounts at state grammars, 15% appeal success, funding figures, international padding, unsupported Sutton Trust/Oxbridge multipliers and political claims. Original value: fill-in annual budget and written-charge questions.
