## Pedagogy and trust

- Concepts precede procedures, with sensible progression from direct calculation to remainders, known shares and comparisons.
- All stretch exercises have supporting explanations in the teaching sections. The ratio difference exercise is harder than the worked examples, but a fully calculated difference illustration appears above it.
- Questions use exact results and do not rely on unstated rounding rules.
- Wording correctly distinguishes amount removed from amount remaining and ratio part-to-part from part-to-whole.
- Original-resource status and scope are explicit. No fabricated official past-paper status, pass guarantee, universal school syllabus, expert byline or measured difficulty claim is present.
- Suggested future improvements: actual bar diagrams, more questions per subskill, printable worksheets and learner testing. These are additions, not promises made by the present lessons.
- Prefer interpreting the heading “A ratio compares shares, not a share with the whole” within this sharing lesson. Ratios in general can compare a part to a whole; the following paragraph makes the intended sharing distinction clear. Optional precision improvement: “In a sharing ratio, compare the shares before finding the whole.”
